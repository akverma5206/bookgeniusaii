Everything a book needs

Built around a chapter-by-chapter workflow, so long books stay consistent and you stay in control.

Chapter-by-chapter writing

Each chapter is generated and saved separately, so progress resumes after an interruption and you can rewrite one chapter without touching the rest.

Continuity memory

Characters, places, timeline and chapter summaries are tracked so the story stays consistent from first page to last.

Real-typography covers

Artwork is generated or uploaded, while the title and author name are set in real fonts, so the text is always accurate.

Professional interior

Title page, copyright page, table of contents, page numbers, chapter openers and an About the Author page from your own details.

Many export formats

Complete PDF, interior-only PDF, front cover image, editable DOCX, Markdown and EPUB, with a KDP preparation checklist.

Private by design

Your manuscripts belong to you. Downloads use expiring links, and you can export or delete your projects at any time.

Genres for every kind of book

Pick a category, or define your own with custom instructions.

Write in your language

Choose the language before generating. Your book stays in that language and is never silently translated. Roman Hindi is written naturally in the Latin alphabet, and right-to-left languages get proper RTL layout.

Write a few words. AI writes your book.

Type your idea in a line or two, in any language. Pick a length and press the button. The AI plans the book and writes every chapter for you. Afterwards you can edit the text, rewrite any chapter, and download your book.

Story style (pick as many as you like)

Writing quality can vary by language. For less common ones, such as Bhojpuri, expect to edit more.

Your cover

This is an AI-written first draft. Click inside any chapter below to edit it, and proofread before you publish. The cover is a designed illustration with real text; for a photo-style cover, add your own picture.

Translate your story

Wrote a story in Hindi and want it in English, or the other way round? Paste it here, choose a language, and the AI translates it. To translate a whole generated book, use the Translate book button under your cover.

AI translation is a strong first draft, but stories have tone, humour and local expressions. Please read the result and fix anything that sounds wrong.

Good to know

Free for users, not free to run. BookGenius has no subscription and no built-in book limit, but AI generation, image generation, hosting and storage have real costs and rate limits, which are set by whoever runs the service. A local, self-hosted model is supported where your hardware allows.

You stay the author. AI output needs your review. Please proofread, check facts, confirm image licensing, and follow each publisher’s AI-disclosure rules. We cannot guarantee that a manuscript is plagiarism-free or will be accepted by a publisher.

Kindle and paperback differ. A PDF is not automatically suitable for Kindle ebook publishing, so the export page includes a separate checklist for each format.
